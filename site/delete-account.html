Delete your account

Deleting your Pipeline account permanently removes everything the service holds about you: applications and their timelines, notes and contacts, connected mailboxes and their encrypted tokens, device registrations, and your sign-in identity. This cannot be undone.

In the mobile app

Settings → Danger zone → type delete → tap Delete my account. Deletion is immediate.

Can't access the app?

Contact the developer (project repository issue or direct contact) from the email address on the account and we will delete it for you.

Also good to know

  • We attempt to revoke the mailbox token with your provider during deletion. You can always double-check under your Google account permissions or Microsoft account permissions.
  • Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your server-side account — use the steps above.
  • The desktop app stores data only on your computer; uninstalling it (or clearing its data folder) removes that data.
