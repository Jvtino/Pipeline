Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-07

DRAFT — review with legal counsel before public launch

The short version. Pipeline reads your mailbox read-only, finds job-application emails, and keeps only the derived facts needed to show your board: company, role, status, dates, and a short snippet (at most 600 characters). We never store your full emails, never send email from your account, and never sell or share your data with advertisers. Ever.

Two ways to run Pipeline

Desktop app (on your device). The downloadable desktop app talks to your mail provider directly from your computer. Your credentials and derived data stay on that computer; we operate no server in that path.

Hosted service & mobile app (our server). The web and phone apps use a Pipeline server that scans your connected mailbox for you and stores the derived records described below. The phone app itself never holds your mail credentials — connecting a mailbox happens in your browser, and the resulting access token is stored only on the server, encrypted (per-record envelope encryption; AES-256-GCM).

What we access

  • Google (Gmail): the gmail.readonly scope — read-only access used solely to identify and classify job-application messages. Google API data use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • Microsoft (Outlook/Hotmail/Live): the delegated Mail.Read permission, read-only.

Pipeline cannot send, modify, or delete your email.

What we store (hosted & mobile)

  • Account: your sign-in email address (managed by our sign-in provider) and plan.
  • Derived application records: company, role, status, dates, a snippet of at most 600 characters per message, and attachment metadata (file name, type, size — never file contents).
  • Mailbox tokens: envelope-encrypted at rest; deleted when you disconnect the mailbox.
  • Device registrations: if you enable notifications, an anonymous push token and your notification preferences. Notifications carry no email content.

What we never store: full email bodies, attachments, your contacts list, or your mail password.

Deleting your data

Delete your account at any time from the mobile app (Settings → Danger zone) or as described on the delete account page. Deletion removes every server-side record — applications, timelines, connected mailboxes and their tokens, devices — immediately and irreversibly. We also attempt to revoke the mailbox token with your provider; you can always revoke it yourself in your Google or Microsoft account settings.

Processors

The hosted service runs on infrastructure and service providers acting on our instructions: cloud hosting for the API and database, a sign-in provider for authentication, and a push-delivery service for notifications. Each receives only what its function requires; none may use your data for their own purposes.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on the project repository, or contact the developer directly.
